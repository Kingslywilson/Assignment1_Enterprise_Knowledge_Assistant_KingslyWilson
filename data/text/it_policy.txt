IT Support & Helpdesk Policy

IT Helpdesk Contact:
Employees requiring technical support must submit a ticket via the IT Portal or email support@enterprise-corp.com.
Critical security incidents must be reported immediately to the Security Operations Center (SOC).

Password Reset Guidelines:
All password reset requests must be submitted through the self-service IT Password Reset Portal (https://id.enterprise-corp.com).
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 90 days.
IT support staff will NEVER ask employees to disclose their passwords over phone, chat, or email.

Network Access & Security:
Accessing company internal networks requires connecting via corporate VPN with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
Unauthorized sharing of network credentials or VPN certificates is strictly prohibited.
Personal devices must enroll in corporate Mobile Device Management (MDM) before accessing company email.
